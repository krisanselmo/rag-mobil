<story>
The sun rose over a tiny island in the middle of the sea. Two palm trees swayed in the breeze, heavy with green coconuts, and a little hut stood on its stilts, with a roof of golden straw.

In front of the hut, Finn, the castaway with orange hair, was stirring a big wooden bowl with his wooden spoon.
"Coconut soup, coming up!" he sang.

Barnaby, the old islander with the long white beard, came back from the palm trees with his round basket full to the brim.
"Here are the best coconuts on the whole island," he said with a mysterious smile.

Above them, Pepper the blue parrot with yellow wings rocked on his golden swing.
"Soup! Soup! Pepper wants soup!" he squawked.

Under the yellow hammock, Clover the white goat was snoring softly, one ear twitching.

It was a perfectly peaceful morning… or so they thought.

* * *

Splash, splash, splash! A little brown rowing boat was coming over the waves. In it sat a pirate in a red vest, with a long black ponytail, pulling hard on the paddles.

The boat slid onto the sand. The pirate jumped out, straightened his black tricorne hat with its big red feather, and waved a shiny golden saber in the air.
"Ahoy, landlubbers! I am Captain Salty, the loudest pirate of the seven seas!"

Clover woke up with a start. "Meeeh!"

Finn put down his spoon. "Hello, Captain! Would you like some soup?"

"Soup? I don't want soup!" roared Salty. "I want the treasure! Everybody knows there is a treasure on this island, hidden behind a secret door in the rocks. And I'm going to find it!"

Barnaby stroked his long white beard and said nothing. But his eyes were twinkling.

* * *

Pepper had been watching that big red feather for a while. Suddenly, he swooped down from his swing, grabbed the tricorne hat in his beak and flapped away!
"My hat! Come back, you feathered thief!" shouted Salty.

Pepper flew straight to the grey rocks and squeezed through a narrow crack. With a long creak, one of the rocks swung open like a door. Behind it, there was a cave, as black as night.

Salty ran to the entrance… and stopped dead. His knees began to shake.
"It's… it's very dark in there," he whispered. "Pirates don't like the dark. Not one bit."

Finn ran to the hut and came back with the lantern. He lit it, and a warm yellow light glowed.
"Don't worry, Captain. We'll go together," he said, and he took Salty's hand.

Barnaby smiled. "Follow me. I know this cave very well."

And the three of them stepped into the darkness.

* * *

Deep inside the cave, the lantern lit up an old wooden trunk with dark metal bars. On top of it sat Pepper, the black hat in his beak.
"Treasure! Treasure!" he squawked.

With trembling hands, Salty lifted the lid. There was no gold inside. Only a pink bottle, with a rolled-up note.

Finn read it out loud: "When I was a young pirate, I came here looking for gold. I found friends instead, and I never left. That is the best treasure of all. Signed: Barnaby."

Salty stared at the old man. "You were a pirate?"
Barnaby laughed. "A long time ago. And I was afraid of the dark too!"

Pepper dropped the hat onto Salty's head. "Friends! Friends!"

That evening, as the sun set over the sea, Finn poured coconut juice from the blue jug. There were only two cups, so Barnaby drank straight from the jug, and everyone laughed. Two sea turtles crawled up onto the sand to join them.

"Maybe," said Salty softly, "I'll stay a little while."
