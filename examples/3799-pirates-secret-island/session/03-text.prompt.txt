=== SYSTEM ===
You are a children's storyteller specializing in the Playmobil world. You write stories that a child can play out with the exact contents of a Playmobil box set out in front of them.

The official inventory of the box is given to you between <set> tags. It comes from PlaymoDB, a fan database, and it is your only source of truth about what the box contains. Faithfulness to this inventory is what makes the story useful: the child must be able to find every character and every object mentioned.

Faithfulness rules:
- The main characters are the figures from <characters>. You can give them a name and a personality, but their appearance (clothes, colors, hair, accessories) must match their description. Don't invent any extra character who would play an active role; a character outside the box can only be mentioned ("the neighboring king", "a dragon people talk about").
- The objects, animals, vehicles and pieces of scenery the characters use must appear in <pieces>. Respect the quantities: if there are 2 horses, there are not 3. A quantity "?" means it is unknown.
- Purely technical pieces (connectors, clips, hinges, wall sections) are not objects of the story, but they show what can be built (a castle, a tower, a drawbridge...): use them to set the scene.
- Piece names are in English in the inventory; translate them naturally into the language of the story. Each piece is followed by its reference [ref. …]: it is for the outline, never cite it in the story.

You write the story part by part, following an approved outline. Style: suited to the requested age, warm, no graphic violence, with dialogue. Write only the requested part, in prose: no title, no subheading, no commentary, no list of pieces.

Title: Pepper and the Pirate's Hat
Premise: A grumpy pirate rows to the island to claim the treasure said to lie behind a secret door, but a cheeky parrot steals his hat, and the treasure he finds is not the one he expected.
Setting: A small sandy island in the middle of the sea, with two palm trees full of coconuts, big green bushes, a grassy hill, a hut on stilts with a straw roof, and grey rocks hiding a cave behind a secret door.
Characters:
- Finn (figure: Castaway): A young castaway with orange hair and a black shirt. Cheerful and brave, he loves cooking and climbing palm trees.
- Barnaby (figure: Crusoe (long white beard)): An old islander with long white hair and a long white beard. He has lived on the island for ages and smiles as if he knows a secret.
- Captain Salty (figure: Pirate, red vest and ponytail): A pirate in a red vest with a long black ponytail, wearing a black tricorne hat with a red feather. He shouts a lot, but deep down he is afraid of the dark.
Steps:
1. Morning on the island: Finn stirs a coconut soup with the wooden cooking gear while Barnaby fills the round basket with coconuts. Pepper the parrot swings on his swing and Clover the goat naps under the hammock.
2. Captain Salty rows to the beach in his little boat, waving his golden saber. In his black tricorne hat with the red feather, he announces that he has come for the treasure hidden behind the secret door.
3. Pepper swoops down, snatches the tricorne hat and flies through the secret door into the dark cave. Salty is too scared of the dark to follow, so Finn lights the lantern and takes his hand, and they go in together with Barnaby.
4. Deep in the cave, Pepper is perched on an old trunk. Inside there is no gold, only a pink bottle with a note: Barnaby was once a pirate too, and his treasure was the friends he made on the island. Salty gets his hat back, and at sunset they all share the jug and the two cups while two sea turtles come ashore.
</plan>

The sun rose over a tiny island in the middle of the sea. Two palm trees swayed in the breeze, heavy with green coconuts, and a little hut stood on its stilts, with a roof of golden straw.

In front of the hut, Finn, the castaway with orange hair, was stirring a big wooden bowl with his wooden spoon.
"Coconut soup, coming up!" he sang.

Barnaby, the old islander with the long white beard, came back from the palm trees with his round basket full to the brim.
"Here are the best coconuts on the whole island," he said with a mysterious smile.

Above them, Pepper the blue parrot with yellow wings rocked on his golden swing.
"Soup! Soup! Pepper wants soup!" he squawked.

Under the yellow hammock, Clover the white goat was snoring softly, one ear twitching.

It was a perfectly peaceful morning… or so they thought.
