At the bottom of the bay, everything was blue and silent. Marine glided between the grey rocks, lighting up every nook. No lantern…

All of a sudden, a little dark blue fish with a pale belly blocked her way. It had a long pointed nose: it was a swordfish!
"Hello, little swordfish," said Marine in a cloud of bubbles. "You haven't seen a lantern, have you?"

The swordfish swam around her twice, then darted off between the seaweed. Marine followed it, kicking her flippers. It led her to two sea turtles, brown and peaceful, resting on the sand.

The turtles raised their heads and swam slowly towards a big rock. Under the rock was the way into a cave. And there, stuck against the stone, something was shining silver and yellow: Pépin's lantern!

Marine pulled gently, once, twice… At last the lantern came free.
