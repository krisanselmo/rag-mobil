A few minutes later, the underwater scooter came back up near the beach. Marine held the lantern up high.
"Here it is!"
"Hooray!" cried Pépin, dancing on the sand. "Coco, our lantern is saved!"

To thank the diver, Robinson filled the basket with coconuts, and Tom brought out the wooden kitchen utensils: the spoon, the ladle, the fork and the bowl. Together they made a delicious coconut soup.

When the sun went down, Pépin lit his lantern on the beach. Its yellow light danced on the waves, and everyone ate, laughing and telling stories of their adventures.
"Will you stay the night on the island?" asked Tom.
"I'd love to!" answered Marine.

That night, the diver fell asleep in the hammock of the hut, rocked by the sound of the sea. And deep down at the bottom of the bay, the swordfish and the two sea turtles watched over the island.
